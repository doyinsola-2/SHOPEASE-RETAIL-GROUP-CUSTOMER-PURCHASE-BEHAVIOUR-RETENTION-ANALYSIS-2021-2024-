# SHOPEASE-RETAIL-GROUP-CUSTOMER-PURCHASE-BEHAVIOUR-RETENTION-ANALYSIS-2021-2024-

This project analyzes customer purchase behavior, churn, and discount impact for ShopEase retail company using historical transaction data from 2021 to 2024.
The objective was to understand why some customers purchase repeatedly while others churn, how product preferences evolve, and how discounts and sales channels influence customer retention and revenue growth.
The analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel, with insights delivered through executive-level dashboards designed to support business decision-making.

## Business Objectives
- Identify products and categories that drive repeat purchases
- Detect customer churn patterns and at-risk segments
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on purchase behavior and revenue
- Understand how sales channels influence retention
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve customer loyalty and lifetime value


## Dataset Description
Time Period: 2021–2024
Records: ~5,000 transactions

Key Fields:
- Customer Attributes: CustomerID, Channel, Payment Method
- Transaction Details: OrderID, OrderDate, Product, Category
- Sales Metrics: Unit Price, Quantity, Discount (%), Total Amount
- Behavioral Indicators: Repeat Purchase Flag, Return Flag

## Key Insights
- Electronics, Fashion, and Accessories generated the highest revenue
- Approximately 45% of customers churned, highlighting a retention challenge
- Customers who returned items and also churned, points to dissatisfaction with product quality, expectations, or the return process. 
- Discounts have no significant influence on purchase volume.
- Website customers showed higher churn compared to app and in-store customers


## Recommendations
- Implement personalized loyalty and re-engagement strategies for inactive customers
-  Provide personalized discount offers to high-value customers instead of broad promotions to preserve margins.
- Improve website customer experience to reduce churn
- Encourage exchanges over refunds to reduce return-driven churn
- Leverage repeat-purchase products in targeted marketing campaigns
